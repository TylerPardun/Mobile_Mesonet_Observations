Analysis protocol fixed before access to the remote composite results

Estimands: the existing pooled-observation two-pass Barnes scalar fields and
T minus N differences at the archived grid points. No equal-case grid-mean
substitution is made. The original full-sample field must reproduce first.

Sampling unit: storm day, 1200 to 1200 UTC, assigned by each storm's t0 in the
case catalogue and checked against embedded cache timestamps when present.
All observations, platforms, storms and outcomes within a day stay together.

Uncertainty: delete-one-day jackknife. Both passes and the first-pass interpolation
at observation locations are recalculated for each deletion. If G is the number
of joint storm days and F_{-g} the deleted-day estimate, the SE is
sqrt((G-1)/G * sum_g (F_{-g} - mean_h F_{-h})^2).
For differences, F_{-g} means F_T,-g - F_N,-g, retaining shared-day covariance.
Intervals are estimate +/- t_0.975,df * SE. df is local supported days minus one;
for a difference it is the smaller supported-day count minus one. Two-sided
p-values use the same t approximation. This is approximate sampling inference.

Coverage: at least 10 effective first-pass-weighted days per group; all deleted
fields must be finite. Effective days = (sum_g W_g)^2 / sum_g W_g^2, where W_g
is that day's first-pass Barnes weight at the grid point. Values outside this
support may be shown descriptively but receive no significance claim.

QC is fixed to the original full-sample 3-km prebuffer and 2.5-sigma scalar
filter. Kernel support is 3*sqrt(kappa) for pass 1 and 3*sqrt(gamma*kappa) for
pass 2. The original treatment of missing/out-of-grid interpolation residuals
is preserved and tested. The comparison therefore isolates sampling uncertainty
conditional on those choices, rather than silently changing the estimator.

Multiplicity: one predeclared family comprising all eligible grid cells in
pgru, theta_v', theta_e', and q_v' at -15, -5, 0, +5, +15 minutes. BY at q=.05
is primary because the covariance structure across fields, neighboring cells
and repeated times is not assumed to meet BH's positive-dependence condition.
BH is returned for sensitivity and is not substituted after viewing results.
FDR adjustment does not repair an invalid primary p-value; sparse-support masks,
independent numerical checks and review of day-level variability remain necessary.
